# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers of StormEngine are expected to communicate and collaborate respectfully.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be constructive when discussing code, design, and technical decisions.
- Assume good faith and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.
- Respect different levels of experience and technical background.
- Accept evidence-based technical disagreement without personal attacks.
- Keep project spaces useful, welcoming, and professional.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

If you experience or witness a conduct problem, contact the repository maintainers through a private GitHub channel rather than escalating the situation publicly.

Reports should include enough context to understand what happened. Do not publish private or sensitive information in a public issue.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other project spaces operated for StormEngine.

## Good-faith participation

Technical disagreement is welcome. The goal is to resolve disagreements through evidence, tests, clear reasoning, and maintainable engineering decisions.
